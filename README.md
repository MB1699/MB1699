## 👋 About Me

My journey into data started with a background in Computer Engineering and grew through 3+ years of working across Data Analytics. From building SQL-driven solutions and BI dashboards to working with Python, cloud data platforms, and machine learning, I’ve always enjoyed turning messy data into something useful.

I recently completed my Master’s in Analytics at Northeastern University, where I expanded that foundation into machine learning, AI, and data-driven applications. Today, I’m especially interested in the space where **data, AI, and engineering come together**.

🌱 Always learning new tools, technologies, and better ways to solve problems with data.  
🤖 Currently exploring LLMs, RAG applications, predictive modeling, and intelligent data solutions.  
📊 I enjoy building things that turn data into insights, decisions, and real-world impact.  
🚀 Always open to learning, collaborating, and building something meaningful.
